My name is Rongxiang Su (pronounced "Rong-sheeyang"), and I am a Teaching Assistant Professor in the [Department of Urban and Regional Planning](https://urban.illinois.edu/people/profiles/rongxiang-su/) at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC). Before joining UIUC, I was a postdoctoral fellow at the MIT Senseable City Lab. I received my Ph.D. in Geography from the University of California, Santa Barbara in 2023.

My research lies at the intersection of geospatial data science, human mobility, travel behavior, and urban analytics. I investigate how people interact with urban space through their everyday mobility, activities, and use of urban services, drawing on diverse data sources, including mobile phone location traces, household travel surveys, digital platform records, and user-generated reviews. I develop computational methods to characterize dynamic human activity and interaction and examine how mobility and access shape opportunities, social and environmental exposure, and experiences of urban services across different populations and places. Through this work, I seek to advance understanding of spatial inequality and inform more inclusive, equitable, and sustainable urban and transportation planning.

I am always happy to discuss research ideas and potential collaborations. Feel free to reach out to me at <ins>rsu at illinois.edu</ins>!

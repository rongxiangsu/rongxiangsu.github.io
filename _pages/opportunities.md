I am not currently accepting PhD students. However, if you are interested in working with me, please read the following.

## Graduate Students (UIUC only)
Graduate students who are interested in working with me can join my research group as research assistants (funding is not guaranteed). For students in the MUP program, you may also select me as your capstone advisor. I will actively work with you to identify and pursue external funding opportunities.


## Undergraduate Students (UIUC only)
I welcome undergraduate students who are interested in gaining research experience in geospatial data science. If you are interested in working with me, please email me at rsu at illinois.edu with your resume and a short paragraph describing your background and research interests.

Below are a few on-campus research opportunities available to UIUC undergraduate students.

[Office of Undergraduate Research’s Research Support Grant](https://undergradresearch.illinois.edu/resources/funding/research-support-grant/)

## Visiting Students
Students from outside UIUC are welcome to get in touch with me to discuss potential opportunities for academic exchange in my research group at UIUC. Please email me at rsu at illinois.edu with your resume and a short paragraph describing your background and research interests.
